# Agile Project Portfolio

**Mvuyisi Mcithwa** | Diploma in ICT Application Development, Cape Peninsula University of Technology
**Subject:** Project Presentation 3 (PRP370S/PRP371S/PRP372S)

## Introduction

This portfolio is a collection of evidence of how I applied Agile practices while
working in a team on **GoShuttle**, a student transport initiative that partners
CPUT with Golden Arrow Bus Service (GABS) to provide dedicated, subsidised bus
routes for students who struggle with unreliable transport. In this project I
worked as the team's **Researcher**, leading the primary and secondary research,
including [interviews, surveys and validation].

The portfolio follows four steps: I **collected** the artefacts from the project,
**selected** those that best show my learning, **reflected** on what each one taught
me, and **connected** them into a single account of how our team planned and
delivered its work.

It contains five sections, each with evidence and a reflection:

1. **Agile Team Charter:** our team's shared purpose, roles, ways of working and
   agreements.
2. **User Stories and Tasks:** they focus on the requirements of the user which these requirements
    became our work to be completed in the sproject.
4. **Product Backlog:** it is the single, ordered list of everything that might be needed in the product.
5. **Sprint Backlog:** is the set of items the team commits to delivering in the current sprint and it is usually 1 to 4 weeks.
6. **Agile Logbook:**  a running record of what happened during the project.

Through this work I have learnt is that working with a team is beneficial because even though
we are in the same course and  developed the same skills but people bring different.Having an agreement 
at the start of the project helps to resolve conflicts or defferences that may come in the future and also helps
to get work deliverd. Each section
below opens with the evidence and closes with my reflection on it.
